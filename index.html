2025 – 2026

Semester project

Aarhus University

Ba+ ion coherence

Modelled Rabi oscillations and noise in a driven barium-ion system, and compared continuous dynamical-decoupling predictions with ion-trap data.

Using Python, I solved optical Bloch equations for detuning jitter and Rabi-frequency noise, and explored dressed-state and phase-modulated driving approaches.

Travelling salesman problem

Worked on a quantum algorithm for the Hamiltonian-cycle formulation of the travelling salesman problem, including circuit verification and runtime analysis.

Universal blind quantum computing simulator

Built a simulator for measurement-based quantum computing using cluster states, Qiskit, dynamic circuits, and OpenQASM 3.

I benchmarked it with quantum gates and common algorithms, and explored a path towards running the protocol on real quantum hardware.
